These terms describe the principal rules currently governing LAJEMBO’s renter and property-provider journeys and the SMS verification service used for account access.

Membership and eligibility

LAJEMBO renter and property-provider services require the applicable membership and account journey. Customers must provide accurate registration information and complete required verification steps.

Identity and account verification

Registered mobile-number and identity controls may be required to access protected services, change sensitive account information or re-establish access after extended inactivity.

SMS Terms

LAJEMBO NIGERIA LIMITED (LAJEMBO) sends one-time authentication text messages only when a user requests a code for registration, login, identity verification or account access.

Message frequency varies based on user-initiated verification requests; typically one message is sent per request, with additional messages only when another code is requested. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of further SMS messages or HELP for help. These messages are transactional and security-related only and are not used for marketing or promotional purposes.

Renter responsibilities

Renters must provide accurate information, use viewing and transaction services for genuine rental purposes, follow confirmed viewing arrangements and keep their account access secure.

Provider responsibilities

Property providers must have authority to offer a property, provide accurate property and availability information, cooperate with required verification and keep listing information current.

Listings and verification

A property must complete required LAJEMBO verification before normal public listing. Misleading, duplicate, unauthorized or materially inaccurate listings may be restricted or removed.

Viewings

The standard LAJEMBO viewing charge is ₦7,500 for an eligible viewing request of up to five properties. A booking is confirmed after the required payment. Applicable decision-window and forfeiture rules form part of the viewing journey.

Rental transaction and Success Fee

The customer is shown the applicable rental cost breakdown before the transaction proceeds. LAJEMBO’s target Success Fee is 5% of the agreed annual rental value and is connected to a successful rental transaction.

Caution deposit

Where a tenancy includes the approved 10% caution deposit, it is treated as refundable subject to the tenancy, move-in and move-out condition records and any properly resolved deductions.

Tenancy documents

Supported rental journeys may include standardized tenancy documents, independent legal/documentation services, electronic acceptance or signatures, receipts and protected document records. The final agreement for a specific tenancy governs that tenancy.

LAJEMBO Exclusive

LAJEMBO Exclusive is the enhanced service journey for qualifying rental properties and searches from ₦20,000,000 annual rent, subject to applicable verification and service requirements.

ShortLets

ShortLet services use their applicable booking, property, payment and stay rules. A ShortLet booking does not create an annual residential tenancy unless the applicable agreement expressly provides otherwise.

Privacy and customer information

Customers must use other customers’ information only for the permitted rental purpose. LAJEMBO’s Privacy Policy governs platform handling of personal information.

Prohibited conduct

Fraud, impersonation, false property information, account misuse, unauthorized access, harassment and attempts to circumvent protected LAJEMBO processes are prohibited.

Restrictions and suspension

LAJEMBO may restrict access, suspend an account or stop a listing or transaction where required to investigate misuse, protect customers, enforce platform rules or comply with applicable requirements.

Issues and disputes

Customers should raise property, viewing, payment, tenancy or account concerns through the applicable LAJEMBO support journey so the relevant records can be reviewed.

Records and service changes

LAJEMBO retains operational records required by its platform processes and may update service features or availability as the platform develops.
